# :lucide-smartphone-nfc: Android solutions

Android's Digital Wellbeing controls are a helpful starting point. Because device makers and Android versions differ, check how your phone handles app permissions, device administration, and accessibility before depending on a blocker.

## :lucide-table-2: Android product guide

| Product | What it adds | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [one sec](one-sec.md) | A pause before chosen apps open | You open apps on reflex |
| [Freedom](freedom.md) | Planned blocks across apps and websites | You want a repeatable focus schedule |
| [Covenant Eyes](covenant-eyes.md) | Accountability and filtering options | A trusted person should be part of the plan |

## :lucide-list-checks: Baseline setup

1. Turn on **Digital Wellbeing** and review your most-used apps.
2. Add Focus mode or app timers for obvious distractions.
3. Protect device settings with a lock screen only you do not casually bypass.
4. Test your setup during the times you are most likely to be distracted.

## :lucide-sliders-horizontal: Digital Wellbeing features

=== "App timers"

    Set a daily limit per app. The app pauses when time runs out and its icon turns gray until midnight.

=== "Focus mode"

    Pauses the apps you pick until you turn it off. You can schedule it.

=== "Bedtime mode"

    Grays the screen and silences notifications on a schedule.

!!! warning "You can undo these yourself"

    Digital Wellbeing limits can be changed from the same phone. For kids, use Family Link so a parent holds the controls. See [Family setup](../resources/family-setup.md).
