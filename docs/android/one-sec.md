# one sec on Android

!!! abstract "Role"

    add a pause before you open a distracting app.

one sec detects when you open a chosen app and shows a short pause, such as a breathing exercise, before you can continue. Its wording and pause length are customizable. Some iOS features are not on Android yet.

[Visit one sec :lucide-external-link:](https://one-sec.app/android){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## Suggested setup

1. Pick one or two apps you open on reflex.
2. Grant the permissions the app asks for and read what each one allows.
3. Set a pause length you will tolerate, then lengthen it later.
4. Check after a week whether you still open the app as often.

A pause adds friction but does not block. Combine it with Digital Wellbeing timers when you need a firm stop.
