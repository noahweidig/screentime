# :lucide-hourglass: one sec on Android

!!! abstract "Role"

    add a pause before you open a distracting app.

one sec detects when you open a chosen app and shows a short pause, such as a breathing exercise, before you can continue. Its wording and pause length are customizable. Some iOS features are not on Android yet.

[Visit one sec :lucide-external-link:](https://one-sec.app/android){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## :lucide-settings: How it works

- It interrupts you when you open a chosen app or website with a short screen, for example a deep breath, a mirror, a reflection question, rotating your phone, or typing random text.
- You choose when the interruption appears, how long it lasts and which type you see.
- It can block apps fully during a session or on a schedule, and set time limits for apps you scroll too long.
- Intention tracking, emotion tracking and journaling support the habit change.
- The browser extension includes an adult content filter.

## :lucide-triangle-alert: Good to know

- A pause adds friction but does not stop you. Use full blocks or Screen Time for firm limits.
- The site does not list which features are Premium only.
- Some research cited on the site comes from a scientist affiliated with the company.

## :lucide-list-checks: Suggested setup

1. Pick one or two apps you open on reflex.
2. Grant the permissions the app asks for and read what each one allows.
3. Set a pause length you will tolerate, then lengthen it later.
4. Check after a week whether you still open the app as often.

A pause adds friction but does not block. Combine it with Digital Wellbeing timers when you need a firm stop.
