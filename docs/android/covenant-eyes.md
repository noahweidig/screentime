# :lucide-eye: Covenant Eyes on Android

!!! abstract "Role"

    add accountability and, where available, filtering support.

Covenant Eyes is a relationship-centered option: decide in advance who receives reports or helps with the setup, and make sure everyone understands the boundaries and privacy expectations.

[Visit Covenant Eyes :lucide-external-link:](https://www.covenanteyes.com){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## :lucide-settings: How it works

- Victory Shield takes periodic screenshots and looks for likely explicit content.
- Screenshots go to your chosen ally only when explicit content is detected. They are blurred, encrypted and deleted after 30 days.
- Your ally also sees an activity report, check-ins and private messages through the Victory app.
- It offers several levels of porn blocking plus a custom block list. It is not a parental control app.
- On Android it runs in the background, text message screenshots are blurred, and private browsing cannot be captured. Your ally is told when private browsing is used.

## :lucide-triangle-alert: Good to know

- Screenshot frequency is not stated on the site.
- Confirm the free trial and refund terms at signup. The site shows a 30-day money-back badge without saying which plans it covers.
- Its effectiveness claims come from the company.

## :lucide-list-checks: Suggested setup

1. Choose an accountability partner who can respond constructively.
2. Read the current compatibility and privacy information together.
3. Install and test the service before depending on it.
4. Set a regular check-in that focuses on progress, not shame.
