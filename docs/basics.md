# Basics

Four ideas cover most of what works. Everything else in this wiki applies them to a specific device or tool.

## Core principles

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :lucide-layers: **Use layers**

    ---

    One tool rarely holds. Combine a built-in control, a blocker, and a person.

- :lucide-timer-off: **Add friction, not guilt**

    ---

    A 30-second delay beats a promise. Make the unwanted action slower than the wanted one.

- :lucide-key-round: **Separate the key**

    ---

    Whoever can change a limit in the moment will change it. Move the passcode away from the device.

- :lucide-repeat: **Review weekly**

    ---

    Limits drift out of date. A 10-minute check keeps them honest.

</div>

## The layers

| Layer | Examples | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| :lucide-bell-off: Environment | Notifications off, apps off the home screen, phone out of the bedroom | Low friction, easy to undo |
| :lucide-sliders-horizontal: Built-in controls | Screen Time, Digital Wellbeing | Free, but you hold the keys |
| :lucide-ban: Blockers | [Freedom](iphone/freedom.md), [Foqos](iphone/foqos.md) | Scheduled, harder to dismiss |
| :lucide-lock-keyhole: Passcode custody | [password-locker.com](iphone/password-locker.md) | Limits you cannot casually reverse |
| :lucide-users: People | [Covenant Eyes](iphone/covenant-eyes.md), a trusted partner | Strongest for high-stakes goals |

## Set a goal first

!!! example "Weak goal vs. strong goal"

    - Weak: "Use my phone less."
    - Strong: "No social apps after 9 p.m. on weekdays. My partner holds the passcode."

A measurable rule tells you which tool to use and when it has failed.

## Common mistakes

!!! warning "Watch for these"

    - Setting limits you will override within the hour.
    - Keeping the Screen Time passcode in a place you can reach on impulse.
    - Blocking apps without a replacement activity.
    - Restricting a child's device without explaining why.
