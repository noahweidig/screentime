# Cold Turkey on Windows

!!! abstract "Role"

    block distracting sites and apps on your computer, with locks that are hard to undo.

Cold Turkey Blocker is a desktop app that blocks distracting websites and apps. It can lock a block in place so it is hard to undo in a weak moment. It runs on Windows and macOS, not on phones. It has a free version and a one-time paid upgrade; confirm current limits and pricing on its site.

[Visit Cold Turkey :lucide-external-link:](https://getcoldturkey.com){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## Suggested setup

1. List the sites and apps that most often pull you away from work.
2. Start with one short block at a realistic time.
3. Test the block, including your essential work tools, before you depend on it.
4. Decide ahead of time whether you will use any of the app's locking options, and who can help if you need an exception.

Pair it with a phone-side tool so your focus routine holds on every device.
