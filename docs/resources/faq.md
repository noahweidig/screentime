# :lucide-circle-help: FAQ

## :lucide-wallet: Do I need a paid tool?

No. Start with [Screen Time](../iphone/index.md#start-with-the-built-in-controls) or [Digital Wellbeing](../android/index.md). Add a tool when the free controls are too easy to undo.

## :lucide-scale: Which tool is best?

None across the board. Use [How to choose](how-to-choose.md) and the [comparison](comparison.md).

## :lucide-baby: Can a child bypass these limits?

Often, yes, if they know the passcode or can reset the device. Keep the passcode with an adult. See [Family setup](family-setup.md).

## :lucide-lock-keyhole: Is accountability software private?

It shares information by design. Read the provider's privacy policy and agree with your partner on what is shared.

## :lucide-hourglass: How long until I feel a difference?

I don't know. It varies by person. Review after a week and adjust.

## :lucide-monitor-smartphone: Do these tools work on every device?

Not always. Coverage differs by provider and OS version. Check the current compatibility before relying on one.
