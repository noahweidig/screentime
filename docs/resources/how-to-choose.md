# How to choose a solution

Choose the **smallest effective layer** for the problem you are solving.

| Need | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Less casual scrolling | Built-in app limits, notification cleanup, and a scheduled blocker such as Freedom |
| Limits that are hard to change | Screen Time plus password-locker.com or Foqos |
| Support from a trusted person | Foqos or Covenant Eyes, with a clear agreement |
| A whole-phone reset | Shift Your Phone plus technical limits that make the reset stick |
| Focus across devices | Freedom on every device you use for work or study |

## Compare before committing

Check the tool's current supported platforms, subscription terms, privacy policy, accessibility needs, account recovery process, and whether it blocks what you actually need blocked. Run a short trial during your hardest time of day.
