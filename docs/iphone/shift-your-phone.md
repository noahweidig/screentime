## :lucide-settings: How it works

- Useful apps such as maps, messaging, banking and email stay open. Distracting categories such as social media, games, news and video are blocked automatically, along with websites.
- You can add apps to a personal blocked list. You cannot mark a feed app as allowed.
- Manual shifts block right away.
- Focus sessions run 30, 60 or 90 minutes, or a custom length. They cannot be ended early.
- Schedules repeat, for example a nightly sleep block. Unshifting is blocked during them, even from your computer.
- If you lose your computer, log in on another one.

## :lucide-triangle-alert: Good to know

- Because a computer controls it, a child or a tempted adult cannot undo it from the phone.
- The site gives different user counts in different places, and its time-saved numbers are unverified.
- Check the current price and refund terms before you buy.

## :lucide-rotate-ccw: A practical reset

- Remove the most reflexive apps from the home screen.
- Turn off notifications that do not serve a person or a task.
- Choose phone-free places, such as the bedroom or table.
- Use Screen Time or another blocker to make the new default easier to keep.

Change one pattern at a time. A small setup you actually keep is better than a complicated setup you abandon.
