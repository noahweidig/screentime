# ScreenZen on iPhone

!!! abstract "Role"

    add friction and limits to distracting apps, free.

ScreenZen is a free, donation-supported app. It can add a delay before an app opens, with a wait that grows each time you open it, and it supports app goals, schedules and stricter blocks. It is also offered on Android; check Google Play for that version.

[Visit ScreenZen on the App Store :lucide-external-link:](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/screenzen-screen-time-control/id1541027222){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## Suggested setup

1. Choose the apps that eat the most time.
2. Start with a short delay before opening them.
3. Add a schedule for the hours you most want protected.
4. Review your numbers after a week and adjust.

Basic pauses can be dismissed. Use its stricter options, or a tool with an outside support person, if you need a limit you cannot undo on impulse.
