# password-locker.com

**Role:** keep a Screen Time passcode somewhere you cannot retrieve impulsively.

password-locker.com is best considered a companion to Apple's built-in Screen Time controls. Its value is not another block list; it is putting the key to your limits outside your immediate reach.

## Suggested setup

1. Configure your Screen Time limits first.
2. Choose a unique Screen Time passcode.
3. Decide who should be able to help with changes.
4. Store the passcode through password-locker.com and practice asking for access rather than bypassing the limit.

## Questions to ask

- Who should be able to release or change the passcode?
- What counts as a genuine exception?
- What will you do when a limit blocks something important?

Keep account-recovery details and emergency access plans documented with the trusted person, not only on the device being limited.
