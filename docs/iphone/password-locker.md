# :lucide-key-round: password-locker.com

!!! abstract "Role"

    keep a Screen Time passcode somewhere you cannot retrieve impulsively.

password-locker.com is best considered a companion to Apple's built-in Screen Time controls. Its value is not another block list; it is putting the key to your limits outside your immediate reach.

[Visit Password Locker :lucide-external-link:](https://password-locker.com){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

## :lucide-layout-list: At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| :lucide-smartphone: **Built for** | iPhone Screen Time. The site says it also works with other blockers such as Freedom. |
| :lucide-wallet: **Cost** | Free for 30 days, then pay what you choose, including $0. A trial signup is required. |
| :lucide-timer: **Retrieval** | A deliberate challenge that the site says takes about 20 minutes. |
| :lucide-lock: **Storage** | Passwords are encrypted with AES-128. |

## :lucide-settings: How it works

- Set up a blocker, such as Screen Time.
- Store the blocker passcode with the service. For Screen Time it helps you enter the 4-digit passcode in a way you will not remember.
- To get it back, you complete the retrieval challenge.
- Offline retrieval saves an encrypted file to your computer.
- The company pays a $20 bounty for a workaround to its Screen Time protection that it cannot fix.

## :lucide-triangle-alert: Good to know

- The FAQ says third-party blockers have workarounds, and the service addresses the Screen Time workarounds described in its own blog post.
- The site does not describe the retrieval challenge in detail, or what happens if you cancel.
- Its time-saved figures and testimonials are unverified.

## :lucide-list-checks: Suggested setup

1. Configure your Screen Time limits first.
2. Choose a unique Screen Time passcode.
3. Decide who should be able to help with changes.
4. Store the passcode through password-locker.com and practice asking for access rather than bypassing the limit.

## :lucide-circle-help: Questions to ask

- Who should be able to release or change the passcode?
- What counts as a genuine exception?
- What will you do when a limit blocks something important?

Keep account-recovery details and emergency access plans documented with the trusted person, not only on the device being limited.
