# iPhone solutions

Apple's Screen Time is a useful foundation, but it is easiest to change when you are already holding the phone. Pair it with a tool that creates accountability or moves the passcode out of reach.

## iPhone product guide

| Product | What it adds | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [password-locker.com](password-locker.md) | A place for a trusted person to hold the Screen Time passcode | You want the native Apple controls to be harder to undo |
| [Foqos](foqos.md) | Structured iPhone limits with a support-person workflow | You need more friction around apps and settings |
| [shiftyourphone.com](shift-your-phone.md) | A deliberate setup process for changing phone habits | You want help designing a less distracting phone |
| [Freedom](freedom.md) | Scheduled blocks for distracting apps and sites | You work best with planned focus sessions |
| [Covenant Eyes](covenant-eyes.md) | Accountability and filtering options | You want a trusted person involved in your plan |

## Start with the built-in controls

1. In **Settings**, open **Screen Time** and turn it on.
2. Configure Downtime, App Limits, and Content & Privacy Restrictions for your goal.
3. Use a Screen Time passcode that differs from your phone passcode.
4. Ask a trusted person to keep the passcode before adding another layer.

Then use the product pages in the left navigation to compare the tools that complement this foundation.
