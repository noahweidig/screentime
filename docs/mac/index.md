# :lucide-laptop-minimal: Mac solutions

A Mac is often where work and distraction overlap. Begin by deciding which sites and apps interrupt your most valuable work, then block them for specific periods rather than relying on willpower.

## Mac product guide

| Product | What it adds | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cold Turkey](cold-turkey.md) | Hard-to-undo blocks for sites and apps | You need blocks that hold in a weak moment |
| [SelfControl](selfcontrol.md) | Free timed website blocking | You want a simple free blocker |
| [Freedom](freedom.md) | Cross-device sessions for distracting sites and apps | You want focused work blocks on your computer |
| [Covenant Eyes](covenant-eyes.md) | Accountability and filtering options | You want support beyond a simple block list |

## First steps

Create a short list of distractions, schedule a daily focus block, and test the block before you need it. Keep a paper or offline alternative ready for the task you intend to do instead.

If your Mac and iPhone share an Apple Account, review the [iPhone guide](../iphone/index.md) too so your limits line up.
